## Key Differences Summary

### Personality Preferences
- **Class 6 Q1:** "When you join a new group at school..."
- **Class 7 Q1:** "Your teacher puts you in a group..." *(more specific context)*

### Aptitude Section (Grade 7 is More Challenging)
- **Class 6 Q21:** "2, 4, 6, 8" *(add 2 each time)*
- **Class 7 Q21:** "4, 8, 12, 16" *(add 4 each time)*

- **Class 6 Q24:** "Ravi has 12 pencils, gives 4" *(subtraction)*
- **Class 7 Q24:** "24 students divided into 4 groups" *(division)*

- **Class 6 Q30:** "3 notebooks cost ₹30"
- **Class 7 Q30:** "1 book costs ₹80, buy 3 books" *(multiplication by 3)*
