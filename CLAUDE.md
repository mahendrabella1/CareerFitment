# CareerFitment - Class-Group Scope Map

This project runs four **independent** assessment/report engines, one per class group. They share almost no code, but a few files ARE genuinely shared - those are the highest-risk files to touch, because a change made "for" one group silently reaches all of them.

**Standing rule: before editing any file below, identify which group(s) it belongs to and say so before making the change.** If a request's own scope would touch more than one group, or touches a Shared file, flag that explicitly before editing - don't discover it after the fact. After finishing, verify with `git status`/`git diff` that only files in the intended group(s) actually changed.

## Graduates (UG)

For undergraduates currently mid-degree (registration category `"graduate"`, stage key `"ug"` - deliberately its OWN stage key, not a reuse of the shared bank's pre-existing generic `"grad"` entries). Built to mirror Class 11-12's architecture: a pre-exam degree/course picker instead of a stream picker, its own dedicated question bank/scorer/adapter, bypassing the generic `scoreAssessment()` path entirely.

Graduates' Career Selector reuses Class 11-12's `CAREERS_1112`/`career-roadmaps-detailed.json` (via `findCareer1112()`/`detailedRoadmapFor()`, read-only) when a student's typed desired career resolves to one of those 308 researched careers - deliberately NOT a separate data file, to avoid re-authoring what already exists. ## Working Professionals (Workplace Stress Check)

Registration category `"experienced_professional"` (label "Working Professionals"), with an experience band asked at sign-up step 2 instead of the student career-clarity question: `"0-5"`, `"5-15"`, `"15+"` (stored as `users/{uid}.experienceBand`). Professionals never take a student assessment: sign-up sends them to `/account/stress`, `/account` redirects them there, and `app/assessment-experience.tsx` sends a professional who lands on `/?begin=1` there too. There is no payment gate on it yet.

| Area | File(s) |
|---|---|
| Question bank | `data/professionals/stress-bank.json` (version `wsc-v4`): **34 questions, ~5 min, the same for every band** - 33 in the **four areas** the client asked for, OneGrasp's own wording, **three answers only** (Never / Sometimes / Often = 0/50/100 - the client asked for 3, not 4 or 5) and one time frame (the last 3 months): **Workload** (11: tasks, deadlines, overtime, after-hours spill, outside responsibilities, no freedom/flexibility, left out of decisions, unclear expectations, poor communication, conflicting demands, exhaustion), **Toxic behaviour** (8: fear-based leadership, gaslighting, gossip, cliques, credit stealing, unfair/discriminatory treatment, harassment, hostile team), **Culture & psychological safety** (7: politics, bullying, not safe to speak up, no peer support, values fit, org change, relationships), **Career & job security** (7: layoffs, sudden job loss, no promotion, no skill development, salary vs cost of living, pay vs effort, no recognition); plus one closing "how are you coping" question (`COPE`: Coping well / Getting by / Struggling) that isn't scored into the areas. Deliberately NO burnout/coping/resilience instruments or extra pillars - the client asked for these four areas only and 30-40 questions. Item ids are stored in results - never reuse one for a different question. |
| Stress drivers | `lib/stress/drivers.ts` - the **14 predicted causes** the results are built on, each a group of questions inside one area (Workload: time pressure, work-life boundaries, control & clarity; Toxic: hostile leadership, undermining & gaslighting, gossip & exclusion, harassment & unfair treatment; Culture: psychological safety, support & relationships, office politics, fit & change; Career: job insecurity, growth & skills, pay & recognition), with what a high reading means and the plan actions that address it. Every question belongs to exactly one driver (tested). |
| Scoring | `lib/stress/scoring.ts` (tests: `npm run test:stress`) - each area = mean of its answers; each driver = mean of its questions (intensity 0-100, "active" at 50+); `contribution` = each area's share of the four (sums to ~100); Workplace Stress Score = 60% the mean of the four + 40% the worst (so one severe area isn't averaged away); levels Low <25, Mild 25-44, Moderate 45-64, High 65+; "Is it the work or the workplace?" compass = Workload vs the other three blended the same 60/40 way, each split at 45 (quadrants steady / load / place / both); harassment or unfair treatment answered even "Sometimes" sets `rights`; `COPE` = Struggling -> `support: watch`, or `high` when the score is 65+; answer-quality flags (same non-zero answer everywhere, under ~2.4 s a question). |
| Words and the 30-day plan | `lib/stress/content.ts` - level and area texts, a note per area per career stage (`STAGE_NOTES`), the quadrant texts, and the action library (`ACTIONS`: each with week-by-week steps and, for most, a copy-ready message/template - priorities talk, deadline reply, boundary message, flexibility trial, role-clarity recap, incident record, meeting recap, HR email, growth and pay conversations...). `AREA_COLOR` gives each area one fixed colour (identity, used in the donut and driver bars; validated colour-blind safe). `SOURCE_ACTIONS` maps every stress point to its actions; `planActionsFor()` = support first when struggling, the POSH/rights action when flagged, then 3 actions for the strongest active drivers (then stress points; max 2 per area); `summaryLine()` names the top 3 drivers, each with its area. Tele-MANAS 14416 is on every results page, at the top when the person is really struggling. |
| Pages | `/account/stress` (start screen or results), `/account/stress/test?band=`; components in `components/stress/*`. **The start screen and the test use the student exam's own look** (`app/examTheme.tsx` - the same `S`/`CSS`/`Stat`/`Radio` `NewExam.tsx` uses): "Before you begin" card, then the exam screen (top bar with timer/answered/marked, section chips per area, question card with read-aloud and mark-for-review, 3 radio options that auto-advance, question navigator; keys 1-3 and arrows). **Results are a dashboard** in the student dashboard's shell (sidebar, sticky top bar): KPI tiles (score, main source area, strongest driver, active drivers), "Where your stress comes from" donut of area shares, area level bars, top drivers, the compass; then all 14 drivers with a details panel (meaning, the question topics it's measured from, "Add to plan"), the four areas (score, share, stage note, its drivers, what helps), 30-day plan (`PlanSection.tsx`), Progress (`ProgressSection.tsx`: 3-option weekly check-in, sparklines, score over retakes). **The results never show the answers the person chose** - only what they point to. "Save as PDF" prints everything. Like the student dashboard, the results stay in their own tab: Retake, Law & Rights, Jobs and the plan's links open a new tab, and `/account/stress` reloads the results when its tab is shown again (at most every 15 s). |
| Storage | `users/{uid}.stress` = `{ results (last 12), plan: { resultId, startedAt, actionIds, done, pulses } }`, written by the person's own browser (`lib/stress/client.ts`; not admin-only). Results from any earlier version (v1/v2 pillars, v3's 5-point scale - anything without `version === BANK.version` and `scores.drivers`) are skipped by `loadStress()` - the start screen asks them to take the new check - and dropped on the next save. Draft answers in localStorage (`onegrasp.stress.draft.v4`) via `scopedKey()`. Career-stage comparison: `/api/stress/benchmarks` keeps one anonymous row per person in the server-only `stressBenchmarks` collection (`v: 4`, band, overall, 4 areas) and only returns deciles once a band has 30 current rows. |

## Per-student progress and the institution portal (all class groups)

- **Browser storage is per student.** Every feature tool's localStorage key goes through `scopedKey()` (`lib/progress/userStorage.ts`) - never a bare fixed key, or students on a shared computer see each other's data. Data saved before this was adopted once per device by the first account to sign in.
- **Progress synced to the account:** feature-course lessons and the dashboard's 30/90-day goals (`lib/progress/progressStore.ts`) on `users/{uid}.progress`; active time by area and day (`components/ActivityTracker.tsx`, areas in `lib/progress/activity.ts`) on `users/{uid}.activity`.
- **Feature home pages** (every class; Money, Legal, Exams, Scholarships, Study Abroad, Research, Startups, Career Library, Internships):
  - built from `components/feature/*`: a hero band with the section's 3D illustration, a search over the section's pages and lessons ("/" focuses it) and a first-visit tour;
  - the illustrations are `public/features/<key>.webp` and `<key>-640.webp`, generated in Canva with the background cut out to transparency; the source design in Canva is "OneGrasp feature illustrations (export)";
  - the tour is seen once per student per feature, stored under the key `onegrasp.tour.<key>.v1` via `scopedKey()`;
  - each section's headline, tour slides and layout come from `lib/features/catalog.ts`: a level path (Money), a course page (Research, Startups), a help desk (Legal), a catalogue (Exams, Scholarships), a decision board (Study Abroad), plus library (Career Library) and listing (Internships);
  - lesson-based sections render `FeatureLanding`, which replaced `CourseLanding` (the old component is no longer used). Lessons open in `LessonDrawer`, and progress is still saved to `progress.courses.<key>`. `?lesson=<id>` opens a lesson directly;
  - `CoursePlayerShell` draws the section's parts as tabs under the top bar. A section's home page is full width, with its menu as a drawer, and the shell links to My Progress.

## Known pre-existing quirks (not yet fixed, flagged here so they aren't mistaken for new bugs)

- Class 6 and 7 never score RIASEC's "Conventional" type - every question's options map only to R/I/A/S/E (verified: no `"Conventional"` string anywhere in `class6Scoring.ts`/`class7Scoring.ts`).
- Class 8's aptitude scoring tallies an internal "verbal" bucket that is never surfaced in the output (`class8Scoring.ts`).
- Class 9-10 runs two different letter-keyed taxonomies through overlapping A-H/A-O letters (8 career clusters vs. the 15-domain catalogue) - `FullReport.tsx` already has a code comment and a `riasecScores` fallback working around the resulting mislabeling risk; be aware of it before changing anything that reads `themes` for a 9-10 profile.

Per explicit instruction: Classes 6-8 are not to be changed as part of any Class 11-12 accuracy/scoring work - their current behavior is considered correct and intentional, not a bug to be brought "in line" with 11-12.
