## WHAT YOU'RE DELIVERING TO STUDENTS

### **A 22-Page Personalized Career Report That:**

1. **Understands Who They Are** (Layer 1 - Psychometric Profile)
   - 8 psychometric dimensions + creativity assessment
   - RIASEC career interest profile (Holland codes)
   - Aptitude breakdown (Verbal/Numerical/Logical)
   - Natural strength domains (8 intelligences)
   - Core motivators and values
   - Learning style assessment
   - Emotional intelligence profile
   - Creative problem-solving approach

2. **Assesses Where They Are** (Layer 2 - Academic Reality)
   - Is their current stream right for them?
   - Which subjects align with their profile?
   - What career pathways are available from their stream?
   - Should they consider changing streams?

3. **Shows How to Reach Their Goal** (Layer 3 - Education Pathway)
   - Top 5 degree programs matched to their profile
   - Entrance exams they'll need (JEE/NEET/CLAT/etc)
   - Required subjects to focus on
   - Recommended colleges matched to their aptitude
   - Skills to develop before college
   - Step-by-step roadmap from now until 20+ years ahead

4. **Validates Their Career Choice** (Layer 4 - Student Aspiration)
   - What career do they want?
   - How realistic is it? (alignment % scoring)
   - What are their alternatives?
   - What are they naturally good at for that career?
   - What do they need to work on?
   - Clear advice: Go for it / Explore more / Consider these alternatives
