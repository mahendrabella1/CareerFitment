### **2. Q20 - Numerical Reasoning (Section 3: Aptitude)**
**Difficulty**: HARD (upgraded from Medium)  
**Domain**: Numerical Reasoning with Profit Margins

**OLD**: Simple 2-step discount problem (20% → 10%)  
**NEW**: Complex profit margin calculation

**Problem**: A retailer purchases for ₹5,000, marks up with successive discounts (20% + 10%) yielding 8% profit. What's the new selling price with 25% single discount?

**Correct Answer**: ₹5,625 (12.5% profit)  
**Key Learning**: Requires understanding profit percentage, markup, and discount calculation combinations

### **3. Q22 - Ratio & Proportions (Section 3: Aptitude)**
**Difficulty**: HARD (upgraded from Easy)  
**Domain**: Complex ratio changes with multiple variables

**OLD**: Simple 3:2 ratio (45 boys → how many girls?)  
**NEW**: Multi-step ratio change problem

**Problem**: Senior:Junior engineers ratio is 4:7. After 12 seniors promoted & 18 juniors hired, ratio becomes 1:3. Total initial engineers?

**Correct Answer**: 132 engineers  
**Key Learning**: Algebraic approach to changing ratios with real-world context

### **4. Q23 - Logical Reasoning (Section 3: Aptitude)**
**Difficulty**: HARD (upgraded from Medium)  
**Domain**: Seating arrangement logic puzzle

**OLD**: Basic logical deduction (All engineers are problem-solvers...)  
**NEW**: Complex 5-person seating arrangement with constraints

**Constraints**:
- C sits immediately right of E
- A not at either end
- B sits second left of D
- Exactly 2 people between A and E

**Correct Answer**: E, C, B, A, D  
**Key Learning**: Multi-constraint logic puzzle solving

### **5. Q24/Q25 - Logical Sequences (Section 3: Aptitude)**
**Difficulty**: HARD (upgraded from Medium)  
**Domain**: Letter pair sequence pattern

**OLD**: Single letter sequence (A, C, F, J, O, ?)  
**NEW**: Letter pair sequence (AZ, CX, FU, JR, ?)

**Pattern Analysis**:
- First letters: A(0) → C(2) → F(5) → J(9) → O(14) [differences: +2, +3, +4, +5, +6]
- Second letters: Z(25) → X(23) → U(20) → R(17) [differences: -2, -3, -4, -5]

**Correct Answer**: OL  
**Key Learning**: Compound pattern recognition with dual sequences

### **7. Q28 - Complex Symbol Patterns (Section 3: Aptitude)**
**Difficulty**: HARD (upgraded from Easy)  
**Domain**: Abstract pattern recognition

**OLD**: Simple alternating pattern (▲ ■ ▲ ■ ▲ ?)  
**NEW**: Multi-symbol sequence with dual patterns

**Sequence**: ○▲ → ▲■■ → ■●●● → ●◆◆◆◆ → ?

**Two Simultaneous Patterns**:
1. Leading symbol rotates: ○ → ▲ → ■ → ● → ◆ → ...
2. Repeated symbols increase: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, ...

**Correct Answer**: ◆▲▲▲▲▲ (5 triangles)  
**Key Learning**: Identifying multiple overlapping patterns
