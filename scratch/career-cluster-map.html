Career Cluster Map

Class 11-12 has ~80 questions across 8 categories. Only 3 of those categories actually decide a student's recommended career domain — this is just those questions, option by option, mapped straight to the 16 standard National Career Clusters.

Mapped (34 questions, shown below): Career Interest (12) · Strengths (12, 2 unscored) · Aptitude (12)
Not mapped (46 questions, not shown): Personality (7), Learning Styles (3), Motivators (6), Emotional Intelligence (4), Creativity (5) drive other parts of the report but never the career/domain match — plus Subject Fit (8), Career Fit (8) and Career Selector (4), which are context questions, not psychometric ones.
